PAVEMENT DESIGN · CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS · LIFE-CYCLE ASSESSMENT · MULTISCALE SIMULATION

Taeyoung Yun 윤태영

  • Research Fellow, Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT), 2009–present
  • Full Professor (concurrent appointment), University of Science and Technology (UST), 2025–present

Research Fellow at KICT working across pavement and construction-materials engineering: design methods, material evaluation, life-cycle greenhouse-gas assessment, and multiscale simulation from molecules to structures. Has developed structural analysis and design criteria for concrete and composite pavements, characterized asphalt mixtures and binders through laboratory testing and constitutive modeling, and quantified energy use and greenhouse-gas emissions over the production and construction of pavements. Builds simulation and data workflows — finite element, discrete element, CFD, heat transfer, and molecular dynamics with machine learning — that connect material behavior to design decisions.

  • Construction material evaluation
  • Life-cycle greenhouse-gas assessment
  • Constitutive modeling and finite element analysis
  • Particle, fluid, and heat-transfer simulation
  • Molecular simulation and materials informatics

Core Expertise

  • Pavement design methods — Structural analysis of jointed concrete pavement, application criteria for ultra-thin whitetopping, composite and modular pavement systems, performance-related specifications for hot mix asphalt, and design factors for planetary road pavement.
  • Construction material evaluation — Viscoelastic, viscoplastic, and damage characterization of asphalt mixtures in compression; PG testing of polymer-modified binders; dynamic shear modulus mastercurves of aged binders; preformed joint sealants; recycled concrete aggregate in asphalt base layers; machine-learning evaluation of binder aging.
  • Life-cycle greenhouse-gas assessment — Life-cycle inventory and impact assessment of asphalt mixture production and pavement construction, energy consumption and sensitivity analysis, environmental impacts of jointed and continuously reinforced concrete pavements, emissions from road-marking and traffic-safety-facility works, and LCA of specialty asphalt mixtures.
  • Constitutive modeling and finite element analysis — Time-dependent deformation, rate-dependent hardening and softening, viscoelastoplastic continuum damage, in-house FEA development, Abaqus, and Ansys Fluent user-defined functions.
  • Particle, fluid, and heat-transfer simulation — DEM for aggregate packing, slump, compaction, gravel-road corrugation, soil shrinkage, and subsidence; coupled CFD–DEM for permeability and clogging; cement-paste flow; heat transfer for black-ice prediction, snow melting on porous pavement, and cooling of asphalt mixtures in transit.
  • Molecular simulation and materials informatics — MD of organic–inorganic systems, polymer units in asphalt binder, concrete carbonation and cement hydration (reactive MD); LAMMPS and Materials Studio/Forcite; Python, C++, Fortran; GNN supergraph models (Graphormer3D), Random Forest, XGBoost, and automated simulation workflows.

Selected Research and Engineering Contributions

Pavement Design and Specifications

  • Developed a structural analysis program for jointed concrete pavement applying a load discretization algorithm, and application criteria for ultra-thin whitetopping on asphalt pavement (2003).
  • Participated in whitetopping design and implementation, the Korea Pavement Research Project, composite pavement systems, and sustainable–perpetual–modular road systems.
  • Contributed to performance-related specifications for hot mix asphalt based on viscoelastoplastic continuum damage models (FHWA, 2008) and proposed design factors and a framework for planetary road pavement (2018).

Construction Material Evaluation

  • Characterized hot mix asphalt in compression — viscoelastic and damage behavior, rate-dependent hardening–softening, and response at high confining pressure — with time–temperature superposition under growing damage.
  • Evaluated polymer-modified binders with PG protocols, dynamic shear modulus mastercurves of aged and unaged binders, binder modification with dimethyl phenol and high-impact polystyrene, and binder aging with artificial neural networks.
  • Developed a performance evaluation method for preformed joint sealants (slip-down failure) and evaluated recycled concrete aggregate for asphalt base layers.

Life-Cycle Greenhouse-Gas Assessment

  • Built life-cycle inventories and impact assessments for asphalt mixture production and asphalt pavement production and construction, with energy-consumption estimation and sensitivity analysis.
  • Compared environmental-impact emissions of jointed and continuously reinforced concrete pavements over production and construction.
  • Quantified carbon emissions of road-marking works and life-cycle impacts of traffic-safety-facility works; conducts LCA of greenhouse-gas emissions from specialty asphalt mixtures (2024–2026).

Constitutive Modeling and Finite Element Analysis

  • Contributed to VEPCD-FEP++, a multiaxial viscoelastoplastic continuum damage finite element program, within a US Federal Highway Administration research program (2005–2008).
  • Modeled rate-dependent viscoplastic hardening and softening of asphalt mixtures, including an overstress-based viscoplastic model with time–temperature superposition.
  • Implemented and verified a linear cohesive viscoelastic contact model for DEM in C++ and evaluated similarity principles and exact scaling in DEM.

Particle, Fluid, and Heat-Transfer Simulation

  • Coupled DEM and CFD to analyze clogging and permeability of block pavement with laboratory tests; simulated cement-paste flow and slump.
  • Modeled heat transfer for black-ice prediction on asphalt pavements, snow melting on porous pavement, wind effects on mixture temperature, and cooling of asphalt mixtures in transit.
  • Simulated aggregate packing and slump, asphalt-mixture compaction, gravel-road corrugation, soil shrinkage and unsaturated-soil subsidence; modeled aggregate transport in rotating drums for equipment design.

Molecular Simulation and Materials Informatics

  • Evaluated polymer units in asphalt binder with experimental data, molecular dynamics, and machine learning; assessed representative molecular-structure size and phase-transition temperature of binders by MD.
  • Developed a GNN supergraph model (adapted Graphormer3D) on a 5,346-case MD dataset for density and energy-density prediction, and a GA–PLS regression model for binder density from molecular descriptors.
  • Co-authored reactive-MD studies of concrete carbonation with micromechanics and of early-stage cement-clinker hydration, and simulations of moisture- and salt-water-induced degradation of asphalt mixtures.
  • Developed COMID to automate bulk-system preparation, crystalline and multilayer structure generation, and tensile simulations (over 140,000 lines of Python); executed over 2,000 LAMMPS conditions on six H200 GPUs.

International Collaboration and Teaching

Maintains research collaborations with universities in the United States, Canada, and Türkiye, and teaches simulation methods and research workflows with technical documentation and instructional materials.
