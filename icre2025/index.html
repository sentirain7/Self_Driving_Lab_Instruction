A Graphormer3D Model with Supergraph for Asphalt Binder Property Prediction

슈퍼그래프를 적용한 Graphormer3D 모델의 아스팔트 바인더 물성 예측

A graph neural network trained on 5,346 molecular dynamics (MD) cases predicts the properties of asphalt binders with modifiers. Slides 1–3 and the last slide (20 of 20) are shown; the slides are in English. The full presentation requires a password.
